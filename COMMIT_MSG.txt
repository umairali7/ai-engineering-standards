Initial import: AI Engineering Standards (AIES) + qualification platform

Vendor-neutral standard for AI-native software engineering, plus an
executable reference implementation.

Standard (Markdown, RFC 2119, human-governed): Shared Glossary + Taxonomy
(phases P01-P16, autonomy AL0-AL4, risk tiers RT1-RT4, roles, CL1-CL4,
EV1-EV6); five modules (AEBOK, AESQS, AEOS, AEAR, AECT); documentation
governance, ADRs, standards crosswalk (CSA/ASDLC/EU AI Act/NIST/ISO 42001),
conformance model, improvement & maintenance plan, six worked examples.

Platform (platform/, Python package aies): executable AESQS -- discover,
qualify, score, review, grant, verify -- with the scoring engine transcribed
from the standard (non-negotiable gates, decisional minimums, scoped RTxAL
grants, human-recorded grants, environment-bound re-qualification);
runtime-agnostic adapters (mock, openai-compat, Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp,
MLX); 12 competency suites (120 scenarios), multi-phase journeys, conformance
checks, env/.env config, conformance test suite.

Status: v0.4.0, module documents at Review.
